# 场景设定提示词（与角色板同源画风）

> **要解决的问题**：角色板是在 Seedream 用加权语法出的——CG 质感、冷调、电影级调色。
> 场景如果换一套写法（比如中文改图体），出来会偏实拍照片，
> 和角色拼在一起就是「真人背景 + CG 人物」的割裂。**所以场景必须沿用角色那套渲染与调色词。**

## 一、关键原则

**画风匹配靠这三组词，一组都不能省：**

| 组 | 词 |
|---|---|
| 渲染器 | `(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3)` ＋ `(Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2)` ＋ `(photorealistic octane render:1.2)` |
| 调色 | `((cinematic colour grading, cool muted colour palette, soft bloom, subtle volumetric haze)):1.3` |
| 材质 | `((ultra detailed surface materials, weathered stone, aged timber, visible grain and wear, realistic dirt and dust)):1.3` |

**光态按场景改——这是唯一该改的地方。**
角色板是棚拍柔光（`soft diffused studio lighting`），场景是现场光（正午烈日／清晨侧逆光／油灯）。
**只换光，别换渲染器**，换渲染器就是换画风。

**全场统一冷调低饱和，暖光只作点缀。** 这样同一集里白天的街景和夜里的油灯戏才像一部片子。

## 二、共用开头（每张场景都带）

```text
(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3), (Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2), (photorealistic octane render:1.2), ((cinematic colour grading, cool muted colour palette, soft bloom, subtle volumetric haze)):1.3, ((ultra detailed surface materials, weathered stone, aged timber, visible grain and wear, realistic dirt and dust)):1.3, ((cinematic lighting, HDR, ultra detailed, 8k resolution, shallow depth of field)):1.2, masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed
```

## 三、通用负向

```text
people, characters, crowds, readable text, signs, lettering, watermark, view labels, arrows, modern objects, western fantasy elements, 2D illustration, anime, cartoon
```

> **空场是硬要求**：场景板里不能有人。挂进视频时人物由角色板提供，场景里再有人就会打架。

## 已出（五张全部按此写法验证通过 ✅）

### 场景一｜神捕司门前长街（正午·烈日）→ `REF-09`

**用途**：EP001 段一／段二／段三，全篇主场景。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a long straight stone-paved street running north toward a government office gate)):1.4, ((high noon, blazing summer sun directly overhead, the stone slabs bleached white and glaring in the sun, short hard-edged shadows under the eaves)):1.4,

((an official gate with a flight of three stone steps and a tightly closed side door flanked by two carved stone lions)):1.3, ((one flagstone directly in front of the gate split by a deep diagonal crevice, loose rubble scattered on both sides of the crack)):1.4,

tiered grey-tiled roofs receding into the distance on both sides, faint dust haze floating in the light, utterly empty street, no stalls, no banners
```

**光态要点**：正午必须写「烈日当空、石板晒白、阴影短而硬」三件套。
只写 `high noon` 会出成阴天或斜阳——本机实测踩过这个坑。
**验证结果**：✅ 石街纵深、两侧屋檐短影、门前裂缝与碎石、石狮台阶全部到位，冷调 CG 质感与角色板一致。

### 场景二｜秋月宗山门石阶（清晨·竹林）→ `REF-10`

**用途**：EP001 段二开场一闪；后续「拜入宗门」类镜头。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a steep flight of blue-grey stone steps climbing from the foot of a mountain to a timber gate)):1.4, ((dense bamboo forest on both sides, early morning backlight cutting through the bamboo, long diagonal blade shadows raking across the steps)):1.4,

((a plain wooden paifang gate with no plaque and no lettering whatsoever)):1.3, the steps polished to a sheen by countless footsteps, thin mist drifting beyond the gate
```

**光态要点**：**侧逆光 + 条状竹影**是这张的识别特征，权重给 1.4。
牌楼要写 `no plaque and no lettering`——否则会自己长出一块匾额，还带字。
**验证结果**：✅ 竹林夹道、台阶纵深、远处牌楼无字、晨雾层次全部到位。

### 场景三｜秋月宗竹屋（夜·油灯）→ `REF-11`

**用途**：夜戏、「夜宿竹屋」类镜头。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a small bamboo-built hut deep in a bamboo grove at night)):1.3, ((inside: a bamboo couch against the far wall with plain coarse cotton bedding, a wooden lattice window on the courtyard-facing wall)):1.3,

((a lit oil lamp on a bamboo stand in the courtyard is the key light, warm yellow; moonlight through the lattice window casts a crisp grid of light across the floor and the bedding)):1.4,

strong warm-cool contrast, bamboo joints bound with rope, the grove beyond falling into darkness
```

**光态要点**：**暖黄主光 + 格状月光**是这张的识别特征。
暖是点缀、冷调是底色——不要让它变成整张暖黄。
**验证结果**：✅ 竹榻与粗棉被褥、窗外油灯暖光、格状月光投在地板上、竹节捆绳细节全部到位。

### 场景四｜祖师堂（昏黄·灵牌）→ `REF-14`

**用途**：认母／祭拜类镜头（原著 seq 5 就是这里）。

```text
<共用开头>,

((the interior of an old ancestral hall of a mountain sect)):1.3, ((hundreds of spirit tablets stacked tier upon tier, rising like a staircase into the darkness)):1.4,

((dim warm candlelight as the only key light, incense smoke drifting slowly through the timber beams, faint shafts of warm light from high windows)):1.3,

heavy timber columns, worn stone floor, tablets face-on but with no legible characters on them
```

**光态要点**：灵牌**不能有可读文字**——写 `no legible characters`，否则会刻满字。
**验证结果**：✅ 灵牌层层如阶梯上升入暗部、烛光与香烟、高窗暖光斜射、地面石板与木柱纹理到位，牌位面无字。

### 场景五｜秋月宗内院（日·白墙青瓦）→ `REF-15`

**用途**：宗门日常过场；同时交代**全女宗门**的设定（人物由角色板提供）。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a secluded courtyard inside a mountain sect)):1.3, ((whitewashed walls and dark grey tiled roofs, a winding stone path leading through a bamboo grove)):1.3,

((soft daylight filtering down through bamboo leaves, dappled light moving across the white walls)):1.3,

unlit paper lanterns hanging under the eaves, no banners, no signage
```

**验证结果**：✅ 白墙青瓦、石板小径、竹影斑驳、檐下无字灯笼全部到位。

## 四、要做成四栏设定板

上面给的是**单幅空场**（直接能当视频挂图用）。如果要在 `设定集/图片/` 里留一套和角色板同样版式的场景板，在场景段之后、排除之前加上这一串：

```text
((environment design sheet collage)):1.4, ((left wide establishing view, center eye-level view, right vertical detail shots)):1.3, clean panel separation, no overlapping panels, cool muted grey-blue background between panels
```

> 但**挂进视频时用单幅版**——四栏版是给人看的参考，模型会试图生成四格画面。
> 早先出的三张四栏版已归档在 `图片/设定板-四栏/`。

## 五、出图与挂图备忘

1. **在 Seedream 出**（与角色板同工具同参数），存 2K。
2. **配套验证法**：把场景图 + 对应角色板一起丢进视频工具跑一次，看人物站进场景里像不像同一部片子。
3. **场景板里不要有人**，挂图时人物全部由角色板提供。
4. **进视频前裁掉标注区**。
5. **一张场景板可以服务多集**：长街不止 EP001 用，`REF-09` 值得反复挂。

## 七、写实版变体（若要走实拍风格）

**注意：换写实就不是只换场景**——六个角色也要一起重出写实版，否则一定违和
（CG 人物 + 实拍场景 = 演员站在 CG 背景前的既视感）。

### 三处必改

```text
原：(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3), (Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2), (photorealistic octane render:1.2), ((cinematic colour grading, cool muted colour palette, soft bloom, subtle volumetric haze)):1.3

改：(cinematic live-action photography:1.3), (photorealistic film still:1.2),
    (shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm anamorphic lens:1.2),
    ((natural daylight, real location, physically accurate shadows and reflections)):1.3,
    (natural colour palette, gentle film grain, HDR, 8k, shallow depth of field):1.2
```

**负向补加**：`3D render, game CG, anime, cartoon`

### 写实版共用开头（可整段替换第二节）

```text
(cinematic live-action photography:1.3), (photorealistic film still:1.2), (shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm lens:1.2), ((natural daylight, real location, physically accurate shadows and reflections)):1.3, ((ultra detailed real-world materials, weathered stone, aged timber, visible grain and wear, realistic dirt and dust)):1.3, (natural colour palette, gentle film grain, HDR, ultra detailed, 8k resolution, shallow depth of field):1.2, masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed
```

### 写实版·神捕司门前（示例，已实测）

```text
<写实版共用开头>,

((a stone-paved courtyard in front of an old Chinese government office, shot head-on)):1.4, ((high noon, blazing summer sun directly overhead, the stone slabs bleached white and glaring, short hard-edged shadows)):1.4,

((an official gate with a flight of stone steps and tightly closed timber doors flanked by two carved stone lions on pedestals)):1.3, ((one flagstone in the foreground split by a deep jagged crack, loose rubble scattered along the crack)):1.4,

tiered grey-tiled roofs and layered pavilions rising behind the gate, thin haze of dust in the sunbeams, utterly empty, no people, no banners
```

**实测差异**：写实版**更暖、更干净**（蓝天、灰白石），CG 版是冷调雾感。
**两套不能混用**，一旦选写实，场景 5 张 + 角色 6 张全部重出。
