# 场景设定提示词（与角色板同源画风）

> **要解决的问题**：角色板是在 Seedream 用加权语法出的——CG 质感、冷调、电影级调色。
> 场景如果换一套写法（比如中文改图体），出来会偏实拍照片，
> 和角色拼在一起就是「真人背景 + CG 人物」的割裂。**所以场景必须沿用角色那套渲染与调色词。**
>
> **⚠️ 2026-10-16 更新：本文档的调色串已按《色调基准锚定》重写。**
> 旧串 `cool muted colour palette` 是审美形容词，不是可执行指令 —— 实测 5 张场景图全部出现
> **绿通道压过蓝通道**（IMG-10 蓝/绿仅 0.865）、**中间调丢蓝**、**饱和超标**三处系统性漂移。
> **新串把形容词换成通道级指令**，详见 `设定集/色调基准锚定.md`。
> 出图后用校验器验收：`python3 设定集/色调校验器.py 图片/新图.jpg`

## 一、关键原则

**画风匹配靠这三组词，一组都不能省：**

| 组 | 词 |
|---|---|
| 渲染器 | `(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3)` ＋ `(Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2)` ＋ `(photorealistic octane render:1.2)` |
| 调色 | `((cool blue-grey cinematic grade, blue channel dominant over green and red, desaturated muted palette)):1.4` ＋ `((foliage, bamboo and grass rendered as desaturated blue-grey tones, warm yellow-green cast fully suppressed, no vivid green)):1.4` ＋ `((lifted blue-grey shadows, clean neutral highlights, no crushed blacks, no blown-out whites)):1.3` |
| 材质 | `((ultra detailed surface materials, weathered stone, aged timber, visible grain and wear, realistic dirt and dust)):1.3` |

**光态按场景改——这是唯一该改的地方。**
角色板是棚拍柔光（`soft diffused studio lighting`），场景是现场光（正午烈日／清晨侧逆光／油灯）。
**只换光，别换渲染器**，换渲染器就是换画风。

**全场统一冷调低饱和，暖光只作点缀。** 这样同一集里白天的街景和夜里的油灯戏才像一部片子。

> **光态例外条款**：烛火／油灯／灯笼等**实用光源（practical light）**本身允许暖色，
> 但必须是**画面里的小面积暖点**，环境与阴影仍守冷调铁律（蓝 > 绿 > 红）。
> IMG-11 竹屋是月夜烛火，它的「暖」是对的，**不要当缺陷去修**。

## 二、共用开头（每张场景都带）

```text
(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3), (Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2), (photorealistic octane render:1.2),
((cool blue-grey cinematic grade, blue channel dominant over green and red, desaturated muted palette)):1.4,
((foliage, bamboo and grass rendered as desaturated blue-grey tones, warm yellow-green cast fully suppressed, no vivid green)):1.4,
((lifted blue-grey shadows, clean neutral highlights, no crushed blacks, no blown-out whites)):1.3,
((soft volumetric haze and atmospheric depth filling the midtones, visible material detail across all tonal ranges)):1.35,
((ultra detailed surface materials, weathered stone, aged timber, visible grain and wear, realistic dirt and dust)):1.3,
((cinematic lighting, HDR, ultra detailed, 8k resolution, shallow depth of field)):1.2,
masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed
```

> **这段是色调锚定串，四句各有分工，一句都不能省：**
> | 句 | 解决什么 |
> |---|---|
> | `blue channel dominant over green and red` | 把「cool」变成**通道级指令**，掐死中间调丢蓝 |
> | `foliage, bamboo and grass … desaturated blue-grey` | 竹林是本片最大绿源，**必须点名** |
> | `lifted blue-grey shadows, no crushed blacks` | 阴影提蓝提亮，防影调塌进死黑 |
> | `haze … filling the midtones` | **对抗影调塌陷** —— 要求中间调有内容 |

## 三、通用负向

```text
people, characters, crowds, readable text, signs, lettering, watermark, view labels, arrows, modern objects, western fantasy elements, 2D illustration, anime, cartoon,
warm yellow cast, golden hour warmth, orange tint, vivid saturated green foliage, oversaturated,
teal-and-orange grade, crushed blacks, blown-out highlights, flat lighting, muddy midtones
```

> **新增的 4 个色调负向项**（第二、三行）必须与正向锚定串配套使用：
> - `vivid saturated green foliage` —— 直接封死绿通道污染
> - `teal-and-orange grade` —— 影视最常见调色，但**暖橙会直接破坏冷调锚点**
> - `crushed blacks, blown-out whites` —— 防影调双峰塌陷
> - `muddy midtones` —— 要求中间调干净有层次

> **空场是硬要求**：场景板里不能有人。挂进视频时人物由角色板提供，场景里再有人就会打架。

## 已出（五张全部按此写法验证通过 ✅）

### 场景一｜神捕司门前长街（正午·烈日）→ `REF-09`

**用途**：EP001 段一／段二／段三，全篇主场景。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a long straight stone-paved street running north toward a government office gate)):1.4, ((high noon, blazing summer sun directly overhead, the stone slabs bleached white and glaring in the sun, short hard-edged shadows under the eaves)):1.4, ((shadowed eaves and wall undersides filled with lifted blue-grey ambient light, cool shadow half-tones carrying visible stone grain instead of flat black, faint dust haze and heat shimmer filling the midtones)):1.4,

((an official gate with a flight of three stone steps and a tightly closed side door flanked by two carved stone lions)):1.3, ((one flagstone directly in front of the gate split by a deep diagonal crevice, loose rubble scattered on both sides of the crack)):1.4,

tiered grey-tiled roofs receding into the distance on both sides, faint dust haze floating in the light, utterly empty street, no stalls, no banners
```

**光态要点**：正午必须写「烈日当空、石板晒白、阴影短而硬」三件套。
只写 `high noon` 会出成阴天或斜阳——本机实测踩过这个坑。

> **⚠️ 本张是影调塌陷最严重的一张**：实测中间调像素仅 **7.9%**（基准 64.3%），
> 呈「晒爆的地面 + 死黑的屋檐」双峰分布，画面发闷发薄。
> 故新增第三句要求**阴影提蓝、带石材纹理、大气填充中间调**——重出时务必带上。
**验证结果**：✅ 石街纵深、两侧屋檐短影、门前裂缝与碎石、石狮台阶全部到位，冷调 CG 质感与角色板一致。

### 场景二｜秋月宗山门石阶（清晨·竹林）→ `REF-10`

**用途**：EP001 段二开场一闪；后续「拜入宗门」类镜头。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a steep flight of blue-grey stone steps climbing from the foot of a mountain to a timber gate)):1.4, ((dense bamboo forest on both sides rendered in desaturated blue-grey, warm yellow-green cast fully suppressed, early morning backlight cutting through the bamboo, long diagonal blade shadows raking across the steps)):1.4, ((cool blue morning light filling the shadows, mist and airborne particles catching the backlight through the midtones)):1.35,

((a plain wooden paifang gate with no plaque and no lettering whatsoever)):1.3, the steps polished to a sheen by countless footsteps, thin mist drifting beyond the gate
```

**光态要点**：**侧逆光 + 条状竹影**是这张的识别特征，权重给 1.4。

> **⚠️ 本张是绿污染最严重的一张**：实测暗部蓝/绿仅 **0.865**、中间 **0.897**（<1 ＝ 绿压过蓝），
> 饱和 **30.4%** 超标。竹林是最大绿源，**必须在提示词里点名去绿**——
> 只写「冷调」压不住，模型的先验里竹子就是绿的。重出时请保留新增的 `desaturated blue-grey` 子句。
牌楼要写 `no plaque and no lettering`——否则会自己长出一块匾额，还带字。
**验证结果**：✅ 竹林夹道、台阶纵深、远处牌楼无字、晨雾层次全部到位。

### 场景三｜秋月宗竹屋（夜·油灯）→ `REF-11`

**用途**：夜戏、「夜宿竹屋」类镜头。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a small bamboo-built hut deep in a bamboo grove at night)):1.3, ((inside: a bamboo couch against the far wall with plain coarse cotton bedding, a wooden lattice window on the courtyard-facing wall)):1.3,

((a lit oil lamp on a bamboo stand in the courtyard is the key light, warm yellow, the warm pool kept small and contained; moonlight through the lattice window casts a crisp grid of light across the floor and the bedding)):1.4, ((cool blue moonlit ambient fill on all surrounding surfaces, deep blue-grey shadows with visible material detail, no overall warm cast)):1.4,

strong warm-cool contrast, bamboo joints bound with rope, the grove beyond falling into darkness
```

**光态要点**：**暖黄主光 + 格状月光**是这张的识别特征。

> **✅ 本张的「暖」是对的，别修**：月夜烛火属**实用光源**——油灯本身就是暖的，
> 这是光态例外条款允许的。要守住的是**暖点别蔓延成整张暖调**：
> 新增 `cool blue moonlit ambient fill` + `no overall warm cast` 就是干这个的。
> 实测饱和 38.1% 确实超标，重出时应把油灯的暖池写小（`warm pool kept small and contained`）。
**验证结果**：✅ 竹榻与粗棉被褥、窗外油灯暖光、格状月光投在地板上、竹节捆绳细节全部到位。

### 场景四｜祖师堂（昏黄·灵牌）→ `REF-14`

**用途**：认母／祭拜类镜头（原著 seq 5 就是这里）。

```text
<共用开头>,

((the interior of an old ancestral hall of a mountain sect)):1.3, ((hundreds of spirit tablets stacked tier upon tier, rising like a staircase into the darkness)):1.4,

((dim warm candlelight as the only key light, the warm pools kept small and contained, incense smoke drifting slowly through the timber beams, faint shafts of warm light from high windows)):1.3, ((cool blue-grey ambient fill across the surrounding timber and stone, lifted blue-grey shadows with visible wood grain and stone texture, smoke haze carrying the midtones)):1.4,

heavy timber columns, worn stone floor, tablets face-on but with no legible characters on them
```

**光态要点**：灵牌**不能有可读文字**——写 `no legible characters`，否则会刻满字。

> **⚠️ 本张中间调丢蓝**：实测中间蓝/红 **0.995**（中性，未达 1.12）、蓝/绿 **1.020**、
> 饱和 27.8% 超上限。烛火是本张唯一的暖源，属**允许**的实用光源，
> 但要写小（`warm pools kept small and contained`），环境用 `cool blue-grey ambient fill` 顶住冷调。
**验证结果**：✅ 灵牌层层如阶梯上升入暗部、烛光与香烟、高窗暖光斜射、地面石板与木柱纹理到位，牌位面无字。

### 场景五｜秋月宗内院（日·白墙青瓦）→ `REF-15`

**用途**：宗门日常过场；同时交代**全女宗门**的设定（人物由角色板提供）。

```text
<共用开头>,

((a secluded courtyard inside a mountain sect)):1.3, ((whitewashed walls and dark grey tiled roofs, a winding stone path leading through a bamboo grove rendered in desaturated blue-grey)):1.3,

((soft overcast daylight filtering down through bamboo leaves, dappled light moving across the white walls)):1.3, ((cool blue-grey ambient fill and lifted shadows on the whitewashed walls, faint mist haze filling the midtones, no warm cast on the plaster)):1.35,

unlit paper lanterns hanging under the eaves, no banners, no signage
```

**验证结果**：✅ 白墙青瓦、石板小径、竹影斑驳、檐下无字灯笼全部到位。

> **⚠️ 本张绿污染第二严重**：实测暗部蓝/绿 **0.928**、中间 **0.964**（<1 ＝ 绿压过蓝），
> 中间调占比 **28.4%** 略低于 30% 下限。竹丛仍是主因，故新增 `desaturated blue-grey`；
> 白墙最容易被环境绿反染，故加 `no warm cast on the plaster` 并显式要求冷调环境光填充。

## 七、写实版变体（若要走实拍风格）

**注意：换写实就不是只换场景**——六个角色也要一起重出写实版，否则一定违和
（CG 人物 + 实拍场景 = 演员站在 CG 背景前的既视感）。

### 三处必改

```text
原：(Chinese xianxia 3D CGI:1.3), (Unreal Engine 5 render:1.2), (photorealistic octane render:1.2)

改：(cinematic live-action photography:1.3), (photorealistic film still:1.2),
    (shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm anamorphic lens:1.2),
    ((natural daylight, real location, physically accurate shadows and reflections)):1.3

**调色串也必须同步换**（换渲染器时，锚定逻辑不变，只是从 CG 语法换成实拍语法）：

```text
原（CG 版）：((cool blue-grey cinematic grade, blue channel dominant over green and red, desaturated muted palette)):1.4,
            ((foliage, bamboo and grass rendered as desaturated blue-grey tones, warm yellow-green cast fully suppressed, no vivid green)):1.4,
            ((lifted blue-grey shadows, clean neutral highlights, no crushed blacks, no blown-out whites)):1.3,
            ((soft volumetric haze and atmospheric depth filling the midtones, visible material detail across all tonal ranges)):1.35

改（写实版）：((cool blue-grey film grade, blue channel dominant over green and red, desaturated muted palette)):1.4,
            ((foliage, bamboo and grass rendered as desaturated blue-grey tones, warm yellow-green cast fully suppressed, no vivid green)):1.4,
            ((lifted blue-grey shadows, clean neutral highlights, no crushed blacks, no blown-out whites)):1.3,
            ((atmospheric haze and natural aerial perspective filling the midtones, visible material detail across all tonal ranges)):1.35
```

> **只有两个词不同**：`cinematic grade` → `film grade`，`volumetric haze` → `atmospheric haze / natural aerial perspective`。
> 其余三句**逐字不变**——锚定管的是通道次序，与渲染器无关。**写实版一样要守「蓝 > 绿 > 红」。**

**负向补加**：`3D render, game CG, anime, cartoon`
**色调负向沿用 CG 版**（同样适用）：`warm yellow cast, golden hour warmth, orange tint, vivid saturated green foliage, oversaturated, teal-and-orange grade, crushed blacks, blown-out highlights, flat lighting, muddy midtones`
